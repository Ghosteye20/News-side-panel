# Privacy Policy – News Side Panel

**Effective date:** 12 October 2026

News Side Panel ("the extension") is a free Chrome extension that shows RSS and Atom news feeds in Chrome's side panel. This policy explains what information the extension handles and what it does with it.

**In short:** the extension does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal information. Everything stays in your own browser.

## 1. Information the extension stores

The extension saves the following **only on your own device**, using Chrome's built-in extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`):

- The list of news feeds you follow, and whether each one is turned on or off
- Your settings, such as how often to check for new stories and the "Unread only" filter
- Recent headlines, short summaries and links downloaded from your feeds
- Which stories you have already read

This information is never sent to the developer or to anyone else. It is not synced to your Google account.

## 2. Information the extension does not collect

The extension does **not** collect or transmit:

- Your name, email address or any account details (there is no sign-up)
- Your browsing history or the pages you visit
- The content of any web page you open
- Your location, contacts or device identifiers
- Analytics, usage statistics or crash reports

The extension contains no advertising, no tracking code and no third-party analytics.

## 3. Network requests

To show headlines, the extension downloads feed files **directly from the news websites you choose**:

- The preloaded feeds come from The Hindu, The Indian Express, The Times of India, Hindustan Times, India Today and FeedBurner.
- If you add a website yourself, the extension downloads that site's feed. If you paste a homepage address, it also downloads that page once, only to find the feed link on it.

These requests are made without cookies. As with any normal web request, the news website may see your IP address and basic browser information. Those websites have their own privacy policies, which apply to their servers.

When you click a story, it opens in a normal Chrome tab on the publisher's website, under that website's privacy policy.

## 4. Permissions and why they are needed

| Permission | Why it is needed |
|---|---|
| **sidePanel** | To show the news reader in Chrome's side panel, which is the extension's only screen. |
| **storage** | To save your feed list, settings and read/unread status on your device. |
| **Access to the preloaded news sites** | To download the RSS feeds that come built in. Only feed files are requested. |
| **Access to other websites (optional)** | Asked for only when you add a feed from a site that is not preloaded, and only if you approve. Used solely to download that feed. |

The extension does not run any code inside the web pages you visit.

## 5. Importing and exporting feeds

You can import or export your feed list as an OPML file. Importing reads only the file you choose. Exporting saves a file to your computer. Neither file is uploaded anywhere.

## 6. Removing your data

- **Remove one feed:** open **Manage feeds** and delete it. Its stories are removed too.
- **Clear everything:** use **Reset** in **Manage feeds**, or uninstall the extension from `chrome://extensions`. Uninstalling deletes everything the extension stored.

## 7. Children

The extension is a general news reader. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

## 8. Chrome Web Store policy

The extension complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Because no user data leaves your device, none is used for advertising, sold, transferred or used to decide creditworthiness or for lending.

## 9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a future version of the extension ever needs to collect any data, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
